Spiritual Awakening

Synopsis:15 year old Oliver Steele may seem like your average teenager. He always overthinks about everything and crushes on his childhood friend, Sofi Sterling. He dosen't really believe he is special in any way. Then Ares 15 takes over power and things start taking a downward turn for him. Loss after loss, Oliver may have to be the most resilient and awaken the inner spirit within him to survive.
